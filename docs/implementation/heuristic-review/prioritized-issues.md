The priorities below come from the frozen 30-case synthetic holdout and the 46-case development comparison. At K=3 the holdout has 3 false-positive IDs and 9 false-negative IDs. Correct expected-empty outcomes are 6/9. Paraphrase hits are 1/6 and cross-clause recall is 75.00% under the published scoring convention. All labels require human adjudication.
